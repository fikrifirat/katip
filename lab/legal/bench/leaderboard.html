KVKK-Bench-TR

Finding and masking personal data in Turkish documents (KVKK). Track cells are F2, recall weighted 4×. KVKK-F2, the ranking, is the mean over real-text tracks; generated documents are a regression check only, since Katip trains on the same generator. Leak is the share of personal-data characters left readable. Baselines: Presidio + spaCy (the standard open-source PII tool, reference baseline) and the kvkk-pii regex library (floor). Speed is measured on the same CPU machine for every row; size is what you download to run it. Version v0.1. Dataset card, scorer and how to submit: github.com/fikrifirat/katip (lab/legal/bench).

Hidden test: being built from 2025–26 court decisions.
