## Steps

1. **Receive and log.** Any channel counts. Log date, person, right, matter. Inbox: kvkk@ornekhukuk.example.
2. **Verify identity** proportionately (Art. 12(6)); ask only for what you need.
3. **Search every store**, including agent memory, embeddings, prompt logs and vendor logs (documents 06 and 09 list them). The masking vault maps placeholders back to people: search it too.
4. **Check limits:** legal professional secrecy and the rights of others (Art. 15(4)); legal claims (Art. 17(3)(e)); national restrictions under Art. 23 **[OPEN: which national law]**.
5. **Answer within one month** (Art. 12(3)); extend by two more months for complex cases, telling the person within the first month why. Free of charge unless manifestly unfounded or excessive (Art. 12(5)).
6. **Tell processors** to act where needed (Art. 28(3)(e)) and recipients of corrected or deleted data (Art. 19).
7. **Keep the record** of the request and the answer.

## KVKK (Türkiye)

KVKK m.11 lists similar rights (including learning whether data is processed, the purpose, recipients at home and abroad, and objecting to an outcome against the person arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems). Applications are made in writing or by the methods in the Başvuru Usul ve Esasları Tebliği; the answer is due within 30 days and is free, except that the Tebliğ allows a set fee for answers over 10 pages or on a medium (m.13/2).
